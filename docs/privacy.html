Privacy Policy

How DIANO handles preferences, scores, and your new tab.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Overview

DIANO is a Chrome extension that replaces the new tab page with a lightweight runner game (or a simple search page when you turn the game off). This policy explains what is stored and processed when you use DIANO.

Data we store

  • Preferences: game on/off, sound on/off, keyboard bindings, runner skin colour.
  • High score: saved locally so your best run persists across new tabs.

These values are kept in Chrome storage.local on your device.

What we do not collect

  • No account or login.
  • No advertising SDKs or analytics trackers.
  • No sale of personal information.
  • We do not read your browsing history or page content from other websites for the game.

Permissions

  • storage — save preferences and high score.
  • New tab override — show DIANO (or search fallback) when you open a new tab.

Sound

Sound effects are generated locally in the browser (Web Audio). No audio files are uploaded or streamed from a remote server for gameplay SFX.

Children’s privacy

DIANO is a general casual game and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on GitHub or visit Support.
